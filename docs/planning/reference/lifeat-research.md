# LifeAt — research notes (2026-11-08)

Observed in Sam's own signed-in LifeAt account (lifeat.io, free plan) during the design session.
Ideas and layout only: **no LifeAt assets, URLs or branding are used in StudyAt** (appplan §6.3).

## Layout
- Full-window background "space" with a top bar (focus mode menu, streak counter 🔥, upgrade,
  desktop app, room menu, fullscreen, account) and a left dock: Spaces, Sounds, Cal, Timer,
  Tasks, Notes, Media, Fortune, Breathe.
- Side panels open from the dock; widgets (timer, tasks) float over the space. The whole UI fades
  out after a few seconds without mouse movement.

## Spaces
- Tabs: Videos, Images, Favourites; a search box; emoji category chips (mountains, café, window,
  flowers, film, autumn, beach, city, books, snow, art, space, gaming, study, …).
- "Featured" and "Recommended" sections of thumbnail cards with a heart to favourite.
- Spaces are a mix of YouTube embeds (muted, no controls, autoplay, looping) and hosted video
  clips. A footer shows the current space's name, author, an audio slider and share/favourite.

## Sounds ("Soundboard")
- Master volume; "Spaces audio" (the background's own sound); a "YouTube link" field with its own
  volume; then ~30 ambience channels, each with mute + slider: Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, Gamma
  waves; Rain; Café; Fireplace; Lava; Ocean; Keyboard typing; Design lab; Office; Cat purr; Wind
  chimes; City rooftop; Restaurant; Street; Park; Jungle; Lake pier; Rain on car roof; Splatty
  rain; Outer space; Birds; Forest; White, Brown, Pink noise; Thunderstorm. Several are Pro-only.

## Timer
- Personal / Group; a "task countdown" mode; Pomodoro · Short Break · Long Break with Start,
  reset and a settings gear.

## Tasks
- Quick add; tags; projects; filter.

## Stats ("Your stats", from the streak button)
- Longest streak (Sam: 40) and hours on app (Sam: 660); a "time spent on app" bar chart over the
  last 7 days. **Focus-time tracking, analytics, Notes and the daily planner are Pro-only** —
  the main reason StudyAt exists.

## Not wanted in StudyAt
Rooms, invites, chat, group timers, calendar/daily planner, Fortune, Breathe, accounts.
