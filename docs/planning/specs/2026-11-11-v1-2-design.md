# v1.2 — specification

> Status: agreed 2026-11-11. Roadmap: V1.2. Branch: `worktree-feat-v1-2`
> (worktree `.claude/worktrees/feat-v1-2`).
> Sam's requests of 2026-11-11, interviewed the same day. Every ruling below is Sam's answer to a

## 1. Premise

Sam asked for:

1. **YouTube quality.** Be able to force a YouTube space up to 4K when YouTube has it.
2. **Undock notes.** Pull single notes out of the Notes widget so several float at once as
   separate stickies.
3. **Bare stickies.** Remove the background of a sticky, like the image widget, so notes can be
   pinned across the screen.
4. **A new tasks system** replacing the sticky-note tasks, modelled on Tweek (week of day columns,
   named "Someday" lists, drag between them), with a compact widget (Day / Week / Lists modes) and a
   full-window board opened like Settings. Better than Tweek: tags, time estimates, subject
   (module) links, and a link to the timer.
5. **Dock.** Spotify moves into the Widgets popover. The dock is Spaces, Sounds, Timer, Tasks,
   Widgets, then Stats and Settings separately below.
6. **Stats widgets.** Live, placeable stat cards with a range switch (today, this week, this
   month).
7. **Research-led study features** (Sam picked four from a researched shortlist): estimate vs
   actual, spaced revision tasks, session intention, and exam dates — which Sam widened into
   **deadlines** (exams and coursework) with a live countdown widget, notes and a subject link.
8. **Countdown chimes.** At 3, 2 and 1 seconds left a pip, then a different chime at 0 marking the
   switch between focus and break.
9. **Background play/pause** for the YouTube (and video) space.

This reverses one appplan non-goal: "LifeAt's calendar/daily planner … there is still no planning
or editing" (appplan §2). Planning tasks on days is now a goal; the read-only Calendar widget is
unchanged.

## 2. What is already true

  whole list; no single delete. Renderer `src/renderer/tasks/` (`TasksWidget.tsx`, `TaskNote.tsx`,

  controller subscribes to `onTasksChanged` and `releaseFinishedTask` drops a done/deleted held task

  `x, y, scale, bare`; `WidgetFrame` takes `pos/onPatch/onClose` and `chrome: 'framed'|'bare'`;

  `START {0.5, 0.3}` + `CASCADE {0.03, 0.04, 6 steps}`; images never auto-hide.

  Quote, Notes + "Add image…".
- **Dock** (`src/renderer/shell/Dock.tsx`): Spaces, Sounds, Timer, Tasks, Spotify, Widgets, a
  divider, Stats, Settings.
- **Overlays**: `OverlayId = 'stats'|'settings'|'help'`, rendered by `Overlay.tsx` (centred dialog
  `min(1100px,100%) × min(720px,88vh)`, scrim, focus trap, Esc closes).
- **YouTube** (`src/renderer/spaces/youtube.ts`, `SpaceBackground.tsx`): IFrame Player API
  (`new YT.Player`), `controls:0`, muted autoplay loop, no quality code. YouTube's

- **Stats**: pure functions in `src/core/stats/`; ranges `SubjectRange = 'week'|'month'|'all'` (no

- **Storage**: sql.js in memory, atomic flush; schema v3; `migrate()` takes a pre-migration backup

- **Shortcuts** in use: Space R N 1 2 3 4 S T K G W M F H ? Esc. **B** and **V** are free.
- **Quote widget** already changes at the study-day rollover and has "Another" (not persisted).

## 3. The design

### 3.1 Dock and Widgets popover (appplan §4.1, §4.10)

- Dock items, in order: **Spaces · Sounds · Timer · Tasks · Widgets**, divider, **Stats ·
  Settings** (Sam chose today's divider look, minus Spotify).
- Widgets popover rows, in order: **Spotify · Calendar · Weather · Quote · Notes · Deadlines**
  (switches bound to `toggleWidget`), then **"Add stats widget…"** and **"Add image…"** buttons.
- The Spotify widget itself is unchanged; only where it is toggled moves.

### 3.2 Notes: stack and pop-out stickies (appplan §4.10)

- The Notes widget stays the stack (paging, dots, new, delete, colours) but shows only notes that
  are **not popped out**.
- A new header button **"Pop out"** (icon, `aria-label="Pop out note"`) turns the current note into
  its own floating **sticky**. The stack moves to the next note. With every note popped out the
  stack shows "All your notes are on the screen." and the New note button.
- A sticky is a `WidgetFrame` instance like an image widget: title = first line of the note (≤ 30
  chars, else "Note"), framed by default, draggable, resizable (scale 0.5–2), **never auto-hides**,
  stays visible whether or not the stack widget is open. Its body is the editable note in its
  colour, saving through the same `noteSaves` queue (500 ms debounce, 5000 chars).
- Sticky header buttons: colour, **"Hide background"**, **"Dock back"** (returns it to the stack,
  at its old `sort`), and close = **Delete** with the stack's in-place confirm.
- **Bare sticky** (Sam: coloured paper only): no glass, no header; a plain coloured square of
  paper with the text; the paper is the drag handle except over the text area; a toolbar fades in
  on hover/focus with Show background · Dock back · colour · Delete.
- New stickies cascade from `START {0.5, 0.3}` by `CASCADE {0.03, 0.04}`, 6 steps (same as images).
- **Limit (call): 12 popped-out notes.** Pop out is disabled at 12 with the tooltip
  "Up to 12 notes on the screen".

### 3.3 YouTube quality (appplan §4.2)

- `SpaceOpts` gains **`quality: 'auto' | '1080' | '1440' | '2160'`, default `'auto'`** (stored in
  the existing `opts` JSON; old spaces read as `'auto'`).
- `SpaceSettings` for a **YouTube** space shows a **Quality** segmented control:
  `Auto · 1080p · 1440p · 4K`, hint: "Asks YouTube for at least this. YouTube still drops lower
  if your connection can't keep up."
- **Mechanism:** for a target other than auto, the player element is rendered at the target's
  pixel size (1920×1080, 2560×1440, 3840×2160 **device pixels**, i.e. divided by
  `devicePixelRatio` in CSS px) and scaled with a CSS transform to cover the window (centred,
  `object-fit: cover` behaviour). The app also calls `setPlaybackQuality` with the matching level
  (`hd1080`, `hd1440`, `hd2160`) for players that still honour it. Auto keeps today's sizing.
- **Live badge:** while a YouTube space plays, `getPlaybackQuality()` is read every 5 s and on
  `onPlaybackQualityChange`, and shown as a badge in the Spaces panel header ("Playing · 2160p") and
  in that space's settings. Labels: `highres`→"4K+", `hd2160`→"2160p", `hd1440`→"1440p",
  `hd1080`→"1080p", `hd720`→"720p", `large`→"480p", `medium`→"360p", `small`→"240p",
  `tiny`→"144p", anything else → "—".
- **Spike first (plan task 1).** The plan's first media task proves on Windows, with a video that
  has 4K, that a 1080p-sized window reaches `hd2160` with target 4K and stays ≤ `hd1080` on Auto.
  If the size trick does not raise quality, the control ships with the hint replaced by
  "YouTube may ignore this." and the badge still ships. The executor stops and asks Sam before
  choosing that fallback.

### 3.4 Background play/pause (appplan §4.1)

- A **play/pause button in the top bar** next to fullscreen (`aria-label` "Pause background" /
  "Play background") and the **V** shortcut. Shown for video and YouTube spaces, hidden for image
  spaces.
- Renderer-only state `backgroundPaused` (store, not persisted). It **resets to playing when the
  active space changes** and on app start. Effective pause = existing rule OR `backgroundPaused`.
- Pausing pauses the space's own audio with it; the soundboard and the timer are unaffected.

### 3.5 Countdown chimes (appplan §4.4)

- New setting **`countdownPips: boolean`, default `true`**. Settings → Chimes: switch
  "Count down the last 3 seconds" with a Preview button (plays pip, pip, pip, then the focus-end
  chime, 1 s apart).
- While a segment is **running** (any mode: focus, countdown, short, long), when the displayed
  whole seconds left (`ceil(remainingMs / 1000)`) becomes **3, 2, 1**, a short **pip** plays
  (new synthesised voice `pipVoice`: 1000 Hz, ~120 ms, at `chimeVolume`). At 0 the **existing end
  chime** plays as today: `chimeFocusEnd` when focus or countdown ends, `chimeBreakEnd` when a break
  ends — so "break starts" and "back to study" sound different (Sam's ruling).
- With `tickLastMinute` on, the ticks at 3, 2 and 1 are replaced by the pips (never both).
- No pips when paused, on Reset or Skip, or if a second is jumped (e.g. after sleep): only a
  boundary crossed while running in normal 1-second steps plays a pip.

### 3.6 The planner (new tasks system) (appplan §4.5, replaces it)

**Start fresh (Sam).** Existing sticky-note tasks are not shown again. Migration v4 renames the old
table to `tasks_v1_archive` (kept in the database, the backups and the JSON export as `tasksV1`) and
creates the new tables. The new `tasks` id sequence is seeded above the largest old id so old
session `task_id`s never collide with new tasks; By task stats keep showing old snapshot text.

**Placement.** A task lives in exactly one place: **a day** (a study-day key `YYYY-MM-DD`, using
the rollover hour — call: the planner's "today" matches the stats' today) **or a list**. New tasks
default to today.

**Lists** (Tweek's "Someday", Sam: "lists can link a subject"):
- name (1–60 chars), optional subject link, sort. Max 20 lists.
- Migration v4 seeds **one list per existing subject**, named after it and linked to it.
- A task's **effective subject** = its own subject, else its list's subject. Moving a task out of
  a linked list into a day copies the list's subject onto the task if it has none (call).
- List menu: Rename, Link subject (or None), Clear done, Delete (confirm in place:
  "Delete <name> and its N tasks?").
- A list linked to a subject with an upcoming exam deadline shows "Exam in 23 days" under its name.

**Task fields:**

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| title | 1–500 chars |
| note | 0–5000 chars, plain text |
| done, doneAt | tick / untick |
| colour | none or a `TaskColour` (row tint) |
| day / listId | exactly one set |
| subjectId | optional |
| estimateMin | optional, 5–600; picker presets 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180 + custom |
| tags | 0–8, each `[a-z0-9-]{1,24}`, stored lower-case |
| subtasks | 0–30 items: text 1–200, done, sort; row shows "2/5" |
| repeat | none, `daily`, `weekdays`, `weekly`, or `every N days` (N 2–30); dated tasks only |
| reminder | optional `HH:MM`; dated tasks only |
| kind | `task` or `revision` (§3.9) |
| rolledFrom | the original day when rolled forward (§ rollover) |

**Quick add** (better than Tweek): typing in any add row parses
- `#tag` → tag, `~45m` / `~1h` / `~1h30m` / `~90m` → estimate, `@text` → the first subject whose
  name contains `text` (case-insensitive, e.g. `@2221`); unmatched `@text` stays in the title.
- e.g. `Lecture 5 notes #lecture ~1h @2221` → title "Lecture 5 notes", tag lecture, 60 min,
  COMP2221. Tokens are removed from the title; the parser is a pure, unit-tested function.

**Done.** Ticking strikes the task through and moves it below the open ones in its day/list. Done
tasks stay where they are (Tweek), clearable per list.

**Repeat.** Ticking a repeating task creates the next copy on the next matching day after the
task's day; if that day is before today, on the first matching day ≥ today. The copy carries title,
note, colour, subject, estimate, tags, subtasks (all unticked), repeat and reminder. Unticking
does not remove the copy (call: never deletes something Sam may have edited).

**Rollover** (Sam: an option, **off by default**): setting **`rollTasksForward: boolean`, default
`false`**, Settings → Tasks: "Move unfinished tasks to today". When on, at launch and at every
study-day rollover, every open task on a day before today moves to today, sets `rolledFrom` to its
first original day (kept if already set), and shows a small "from Tue 10 Nov" marker. When off,
nothing moves.

**Reminders** (Sam: yes). A dated, open task with `reminder` fires a Windows notification
"Reminder · <title>" at that wall-clock time on its study day — a time before the rollover hour
falls on the next calendar date (call). Fires once per task-day. Missed while the app was closed:
fired at launch if ≤ 60 min late, otherwise dropped (call). Clicking it shows the window and opens
the task. Reminders follow the existing **`notifications`** setting (call). Scheduled in main.

**Delete.** Task detail menu → Delete, with an in-place confirm. Deleting a task held by the timer
behaves as today (released when no focus segment is open; session rows keep their snapshot).

### 3.7 Planner views (appplan §4.5)

**Widget** (dock Tasks button, `T`; `WidgetId 'tasks'` kept): header tabs **Day · Week · Lists**,
a filter button (subject, tag) and **Expand** (opens the board, also **B**).
- **Day**: the day's tasks (open, then done) with ‹ › to move a day and a Today button; quick-add
  row at the bottom; the day's deadlines as chips at the top.
- **Week**: Mon–Sun sections (collapsible, with open counts), quick-add per day; drag between days.
- **Lists**: a list picker and that list's tasks, quick-add row.
- **Row**: checkbox, title, then chips: subject colour dot, estimate vs actual ("32m / 45m", amber
  once actual > estimate), subtasks "2/5", tags, repeat ↻, reminder bell; on hover **▶** and drag
  grip. Click opens the task detail popover.

**Board** (new `OverlayId 'tasks'`, opened by Expand or **B**, closed by Esc) — the Overlay shell at
**full size** (inset 24 px; Settings and Stats keep their size). Header: "November 2026 W46",
view switch **Day · Week · Month**, ‹ Today ›, filter, "+ New task".
- **Week** (Tweek layout): Mon–Fri columns, then **Sat and Sun stacked** in a sixth column; each
  column is ruled rows with a quick-add on the first empty row. Each day header shows
  "<planned> planned · <studied> studied" (planned = sum of open+done estimates; studied = focus
  time that day, focus-only). Deadlines show as chips under the header. **Below**: the lists, three
  per row, each with its name (editable inline), subject chip, tasks and a quick-add row, plus
  "+ New list". Drag and drop between any day and list, and reorder within one.
- **Day**: the chosen day's tasks large, with notes, subtasks and estimate vs actual expanded;
  the lists in a right-hand column to drag from.
- **Month**: Mon-start grid; each cell shows the date, up to 3 task titles + "+n more", deadline
  chips, and studied time; today highlighted. Click a cell → Day view of it; drop a task on a cell
  → move it there.
- Drag and drop uses `@dnd-kit/core` + `@dnd-kit/sortable` (pure JS, keyboard-accessible; call).
  Keyboard alternative everywhere: the detail popover's placement picker.

**Task detail popover** (widget and board, Tweek-style): placement chip (day picker / list picker),
title, done check, note, subtasks (add, tick, reorder, delete), subject select, estimate select,
tags (chips + input), colour, repeat, reminder (dated only), "Focused: 32m" (actual), menu:
**Start focus** · **Schedule revision…** · Duplicate · Delete.

### 3.8 Tasks and the timer (appplan §4.4)

- **Picker** (`TaskSelect`): "No task", then groups **Today**, **This week**, **Lists** (open tasks
  only), with a type-to-filter box. The held task is still shown by snapshot text when done or
  deleted (as v1.1).
- **Auto subject (Sam).** Picking a task whose effective subject differs from the timer's subject
  also sets the subject. Task and subject change as **one** session split (one closed row, one new
  row), not two. Sam can change the subject afterwards.
- **▶ on a task (Sam: set task + start focus):** sets task and subject, then
  - timer idle in a focus mode → starts it;
  - timer idle in a break mode → switches to Focus and starts it;
  - focus running or paused → only sets the task (one split, as above);
  - a break running → sets the task for the next focus and shows "Task set for your next focus."
- **Actual** = sum of `focusMs` of sessions with that `task_id`, including the running row live.
  Served with the task list; refreshed on segment end and on each focus minute (as the overview).

### 3.9 Spaced revision (research: Dunlosky et al. 2013; Cepeda et al. 2008)

- Settings → Tasks: **`revisionTags: string[]`, default `['lecture']`**, and
  **`revisionDays: number[]`, default `[1, 3, 7, 21]`** (1–4 values, each 1–90).
- Ticking a non-revision task that has a revision tag shows an inline bar in that widget/board:
  "Schedule revision? **1 · 3 · 7 · 21 days**  [Add] [Not now]" (day chips toggleable). It stays
  until answered, another task is ticked, or the view closes.
- The task menu's "Schedule revision…" opens the same bar for any task (manual).
- **Add** creates, for each chosen n, a task "Revise: <title>" on (tick day + n), `kind 'revision'`,
  same subject, tag `revision`, no estimate, `originTaskId` = the task.
- **Exam cap:** if the effective subject has an upcoming exam deadline, reviews after the exam's
  day are dropped; if any were dropped and no review falls on the day before the exam, one is added
  there (if that day is after the tick day).
- Revision tasks never offer revision again.

### 3.10 Deadlines (exams and coursework)

- Table `deadlines`: title (1–120), kind `exam | coursework`, subject (optional), due date + time
  (stored as a local wall-clock `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM`; default time 09:00 for exams, 12:00 for
  coursework — call), note (0–5000), done.
- **Deadlines widget** (`WidgetId 'deadlines'`, single, in the Widgets popover): upcoming open
  deadlines soonest first (scrolls past 8), "+ Add" in the header, a "Past" toggle (last 10).
  Row: "Exam" / "CW" badge, title, subject colour dot, **live countdown**:
  ≥ 48 h "23d 4h"; < 48 h "31h 12m"; < 1 h "42m 08s" (updates every second); past due "Due" then
  hidden from upcoming. Click → detail: edit fields, note, **Mark done**, Delete (in-place
  confirm). Supports bare mode.
- Deadlines also appear as chips on their day in the board (Week, Day, Month) and the widget's
  Day view, and feed the exam cap (§3.9) and the list "Exam in N days" line (§3.6).
- Countdowns use the wall clock (not accounting); a clock change just re-renders.

### 3.11 Session intention (research: implementation intentions)

- Setting **`sessionIntention: boolean`, default `true`**, Settings → Timer: "Ask what I'll do
  each focus".
- In focus modes the timer widget shows a one-line field under the task picker:
  placeholder "By the end I will…" (0–140 chars). Picking a task fills it with the task title if
  the field is empty.
- The intention is held by the controller (`TimerState.intention`) and written to the session rows
  of the current focus segment (`sessions.intention`). Editing it mid-segment updates the open row
  and later rows; it never splits a row.
- When a focus segment ends (completes, or is skipped/reset) with ≥ 1 min of focus logged and an
  intention set, the timer widget shows "Did you do it? **Done · Partly · Not**" and a Skip link,
  until answered or the next focus starts. The answer is stored as `sessions.outcome`
  (`done|partly|not`) on every row of that pomodoro. Then the field clears.
- The Stats sessions table shows the intention and an outcome mark.

### 3.12 Stats widgets (appplan §4.7, §4.10)

- Up to **6** stats widgets, added from the Widgets popover ("Add stats widget…" adds a
  **Total time · This week** card, cascaded like images). Each is a `WidgetFrame` instance with
  card, range, position, scale and bare mode stored in table `stat_widgets`. Never auto-hide.
- Header: the card name and a range switch **Today · Week · Month**; menu: change card, hide
  background, remove.
- **Cards:**
  - **Total time** — large total for the range; under it the change vs **the same elapsed part of
    the previous period** (today vs yesterday by this time of day; this week Mon→today vs last week
    Mon→same weekday; this month 1st→today vs last month 1st→same date, clamped), e.g.
    "+12% vs last week". Today ticks live every second while focus runs.
  - **By subject** — horizontal bars per subject for the range (top 6 + "Other"), minutes labels,
    subject colours.
  - **Goal + streak** — Today: the daily goal ring and "🔥 N-day streak"; Week/Month: "Goal hit
    k of n days" with a dot per day, and the streak.
  - **Estimates** — for done tasks with an estimate whose day is in the range: planned total vs
    focused total, "Tasks took 1.3× your estimates", the 3 biggest over-runs, and a per-subject ratio
    line (e.g. "COMP2261 · 1.6×"). "No estimated tasks yet" when empty.
- Data from a new `stats:card(card, range)` IPC computed in main by pure functions in
  `src/core/stats/` (new range `today`). Refetched on each focus minute, on segment end and on
  session edits; the Today total adds the live running segment each second from timer state.

## 5. Data and IPC

**Migration v4** (one migration, pre-migration backup as always):

- `ALTER TABLE tasks RENAME TO tasks_v1_archive`.
- `task_lists(id INTEGER PK AUTOINCREMENT, name TEXT NOT NULL, subject_id INTEGER NULL, sort
  INTEGER NOT NULL, created_at INTEGER NOT NULL)`; seeded with one row per subject.
- `tasks(id INTEGER PK AUTOINCREMENT, title, note, done, done_at, colour NULL, day TEXT NULL,
  list_id INTEGER NULL, subject_id NULL, estimate_min NULL, repeat TEXT NULL (JSON), reminder TEXT
  NULL, reminded_day TEXT NULL, kind TEXT CHECK (kind IN ('task','revision')) DEFAULT 'task',
  origin_task_id NULL, rolled_from TEXT NULL, sort, created_at, updated_at,
  CHECK ((day IS NULL) <> (list_id IS NULL)))`; `sqlite_sequence` for `tasks` set to
  `max(old id)`.
- `task_tags(task_id, tag, PRIMARY KEY(task_id, tag))`; `subtasks(id PK, task_id, text, done,
  sort)`; deleting a task deletes its tags and subtasks.
- `notes` + `popped INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0, x REAL NULL, y REAL NULL, scale REAL NOT NULL
  DEFAULT 1, bare INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`.
- `deadlines(id PK, title, kind CHECK IN ('exam','coursework'), subject_id NULL, due TEXT NOT NULL,
  note, done, created_at, updated_at)`.
- `stat_widgets(id PK, card CHECK IN ('total','subjects','goal','estimates'), range CHECK IN
  ('today','week','month'), x REAL, y REAL, scale REAL DEFAULT 1, bare INTEGER DEFAULT 0,
  created_at)`.
- `sessions` + `intention TEXT NULL`, `outcome TEXT NULL CHECK (outcome IN ('done','partly','not'))`.
- Settings (JSON, defaults merged): `countdownPips: true`, `rollTasksForward: false`,
  `sessionIntention: true`, `revisionTags: ['lecture']`, `revisionDays: [1,3,7,21]`;
  `WidgetId` += `'deadlines'` (closed by default).

**IPC** (typed in `src/shared/ipc.ts`, validated in `src/main/ipc.ts`; every write returns what
the renderer needs to re-render without a second call):

- `planner:week(weekStartKey)`, `planner:month(monthKey)`, `planner:day(dayKey)`,
  `planner:lists()` → tasks with tags, subtasks, effective subject and `actualMs`, plus deadlines
  and studied ms per day for the range.

## 6. Accounting impact

No rule in appplan §5 changes. Nothing here creates, extends, pauses or deletes focus time:
- task/subject auto-set uses the existing split (rule 5, v1.1 ruling A3), merged into one split;
- intention/outcome are labels on existing rows; editing the intention never splits a row;
- "actual" and stats cards only read focus time; "studied" per day is the same day total stats use;
- deadlines, reminders, pips and revision tasks touch no session.

## 7. Failure and edge cases

- **Migration** fails → no change (transaction) and the pre-migration backup exists; a v1.1 build
  opening a v4 file shows the existing "newer database" error.
- **Rollover across a closed app / sleep / clock change:** rolling forward runs at launch and on the
  date-watch event; it is idempotent (only days < today move).
- **DST:** day keys are strings; repeat and revision dates are computed on day keys, not ms.
  Reminders resolve wall-clock times with local time on the day.
- **Task deleted while held / ▶ on a deleted task:** "That task no longer exists." and the list
  refreshes.
- **Subject deleted:** lists and tasks linked to it fall back to no subject; deadlines too.
- **Drag dropped outside any target** → nothing moves. Concurrent edits are single-user; last write
  wins.
- **YouTube offline / embed blocked:** unchanged notices; badge shows "—". 4K on a slow line:
  YouTube lowers it; the badge tells the truth.
- **Pips after sleep or when the window is hidden:** only real 1-second crossings play (§3.5).
- **Limits:** 20 lists, 12 popped notes, 6 stats widgets, 8 tags, 30 subtasks; the controls disable
  at the limit with a tooltip.
- **Huge week** (many tasks in a day): columns scroll inside themselves; the board never overflows
  the window.

## 10. Hand-walk (Sam, in the running app)

1. Check the dock: no Spotify; open Widgets → turn Spotify on and off.
2. Notes: make three notes, pop two out, drag them apart, hide one's background, restart the app —
   both stickies are where you left them; dock one back.
3. Pick a YouTube space with 4K, set Quality → 4K, watch the badge climb to 2160p; press V to pause
   and again to play; switch space and check it plays.
4. Set focus to 1 min, start, listen for pip · pip · pip · focus chime; let the break run to its end
   and hear pip · pip · pip · break chime.
5. Press B: board opens. Quick-add `Lecture 5 notes #lecture ~1h @2221` on today; drag a task from
   the COMP2221 list to Thursday; switch to Month and Day views.
6. Press ▶ on the lecture task: focus starts with COMP2221 and the task set; type an intention;
   skip after a minute and answer "Partly".
7. Tick the lecture task: accept "Schedule revision" and find the Revise tasks on the board.
8. Add an exam for COMP2221 next month and a coursework due tomorrow; turn on the Deadlines widget
   and watch the countdowns; see "Exam in N days" on the COMP2221 list.
9. Add three stats widgets (Total · Today, By subject · Week, Estimates · Month); run a focus and
   watch Today tick; hide one's background.
10. Settings → Tasks: turn on "Move unfinished tasks to today", leave a task on yesterday (or change
    the date), relaunch, check it moved with "from …".
11. Give a task a reminder two minutes ahead and wait for the notification.
